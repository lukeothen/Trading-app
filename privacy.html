FENCE MATE · PUBLIC BETA

Privacy notice

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Important beta limitations

Fence Mate is an early test version. Guest users keep saved jobs, notes, estimates and photos in their browser on this device. If account services have been configured and you create an account, saved jobs, notes and estimates are synced to your account; photos remain local to the device in this beta. Do not enter sensitive personal information or information you do not have permission to store.

Information stored on your device

When you use job features, the app stores local copies of jobs, notes, estimates and related data in your browser. Photos are stored as browser data on your device. If you sign in, job records, notes and estimates are synced to the Fence Mate Supabase database and associated with your account. Row-level security is configured so an authenticated user can access only their own job records. Photos are not synced to the cloud in this beta. Job data is separate from activity analytics.

Optional usage analytics

Analytics is off unless you actively choose “Allow analytics” in the app. If enabled, the app sends a randomly generated browser identifier, event category, feature label and event time to the Fence Mate Supabase database. It is intended to measure feature use and returning browsers. It does not include your job notes, photos, measurements, AI prompts, AI answers or estimate contents. You can turn analytics off using the same controls in the app. Clearing browser storage also removes the local analytics preference and identifier.

AI Mate

If you use AI Mate, the question you type is sent from the app's server-side function to Google's Gemini AI service to generate a response. Do not include customer names, addresses, contact details or confidential job information. The app does not intentionally include AI questions or answers in usage analytics. Google's processing of prompts is subject to Google's own terms and privacy information.

Supplier search

If you choose to open an AVS product search, your browser visits AVS's website. That site has its own privacy practices. Fence Mate does not guarantee live supplier prices; confirm the exact item, VAT basis, availability and delivery with the supplier.

Security and retention

Analytics requests are sent through a server-side function; the Supabase secret is not exposed in browser code. Account sign-in uses Supabase Auth and a public anonymous project key; access to cloud job rows is restricted by database row-level security. Analytics records and account job data may be retained in the project's database while the beta is being evaluated. This notice does not yet specify a final retention period.

Your choices and questions

Analytics is optional and can be switched off in the app. For access, deletion or other privacy requests, contact the Fence Mate beta operator using the contact method provided with your beta invitation. Before a wider public launch, the operator should publish a direct, monitored contact address and confirm the applicable retention period and privacy arrangements.

Fence Mate provides estimates only. Verify calculations, site conditions and product specifications before ordering materials or quoting a customer.
