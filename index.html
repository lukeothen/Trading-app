How Fence Mate works

From measurements to a materials list in a few simple steps.

01 · Start a calculation

Choose the fence type, enter the run and set the options that match the job.

02 · Review your quantities

Check the calculated materials against the actual site, corners, gates and ground conditions.

03 · Price your materials

Open a saved job, find an AVS product for each line, and select a matching product when available. If you cancel or no match is found, keep the material and enter a price manually.

04 · Save and quote

Keep job notes and photos, build an estimate, then share or copy the customer quote.
