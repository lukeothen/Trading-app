AVS PRICING & ORDERING — BETA

The best way to get started is to use the Materials Calculator to work out what you need for your fencing job and create your materials shopping list.

  • Create your shopping list using the Materials Calculator.
  • Search AVS products and view matching product listings and prices.
  • Add prices to your estimate and calculate your material costs.
  • Add your profit, labour and other costs to prepare a customer quote.
  • Order directly through AVS by visiting their website or contacting them.

Direct ordering through FENCE MATE isn’t available yet, but you can use the app to plan your job, price your materials and prepare your quote before ordering.

Please check all quantities, product specifications, current prices, VAT and delivery costs before placing an order.

Try to find matching public AVS listings for each material. If automatic lookup is blocked or no match appears, open AVS search and enter the verified price manually. Confirm the exact specification, VAT basis and delivery before quoting.

QUICK GUIDE

How Fence Mate works

From measurements to a materials list in a few simple steps.

01 · Start a calculation

Choose the fence type, enter the run and set the options that match the job.

02 · Review your quantities

Check the calculated materials against the actual site, corners, gates and ground conditions.

03 · Price your materials

Open a saved job, find an AVS product for each line, and select a matching product when available. If you cancel or no match is found, keep the material and enter a price manually.

04 · Save and quote

Keep job notes and photos, build an estimate, then share or copy the customer quote.

Calculations are estimates. Confirm product specifications, stock, VAT, delivery and site measurements before ordering or quoting.
